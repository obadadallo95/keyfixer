Privacy policy

Updated August 1, 2026

Your text stays yours

KeyFixer processes text entirely on your device. It does not send text, browsing history, or personal information to any server.

What is stored

Only your keyboard, conversion mode, and interface language preferences are stored locally. Typed and corrected text is never saved.

Why permissions are needed

  • Context menus: show KeyFixer when you right-click selected text.
  • Active tab and scripting: run only after you choose a KeyFixer command, on that page.
  • Storage: remember preferences on this device.
  • Clipboard: copy the corrected result when requested.
The extension contains no analytics, advertising, accounts, or third-party network services.
